# Financial Governance

Maintaining liquidity and profitability is the core responsibility of the board.

## 1. Capital Allocation Policy
Cash reserves must be maintained to cover 6 months of operational overhead at all times. Investment of surplus capital requires a ROI projection of at least 15%.

## 2. Project Budget Approval Limits
*   **Standard Projects**: Budget approved by Managing Director.
*   **Mega-Infrastructure (> $5M)**: Full Board review of financial feasibility study.
*   **Variation Orders**: Any variation order exceeding 10% of the original contract value must be approved by the Financial Director.

## 3. Margin Thresholds
For all architecture projects, the following net margin benchmarks apply:
*   **Residential/Boutique**: 25% - 30%
*   **Commercial Infrastructure**: 15% - 20%
*   **Government Tenders**: 10% - 12%

## 4. Vendor Payment Authorization
*   All payments > $10k must be dual-signed (PM + Director).
*   Payment cycles are fixed at net 30 days unless a strategic discount is negotiated.

## 5. Profitability Benchmarks
Quarterly reviews will assess:
*   **PPR (Project Profitability Ratio)**
*   **WIP (Work In Progress) Valuation**
*   **DSO (Days Sales Outstanding)**: Target < 45 days.
