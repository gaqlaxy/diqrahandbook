# Supply Chain & Vendor Governance

The reliability of Diqra depends on the integrity of our partners and the quality of our materials.

## 1. Strategic Partner Prequalification
All Tier-1 consultants and material suppliers must undergo a biennial "Strategic Audit" covering:
*   Financial stability.
*   Quality management (ISO 9001).
*   Lead-time reliability history.

## 2. Material Specification Audit
*   Architecture Review Board (ARB) must approve all primary structural materials.
*   "Certificate of Authenticity" required for all specialty steel and facade elements.

## 3. Procurement Ethical Standards
*   **Whistleblower Policy**: Suppliers have access to an independent hotline for reporting unethical procurement practices.
*   **Prompt Payment**: Diqra commits to paying small-scale specialized artisans within 15 days of verified delivery.

## 4. Conflict Resolution with Vendors
Every major supply contract must include an "Executive Escalation" clause, requiring direct negotiation between Director-level leadership before entering legal arbitration.
