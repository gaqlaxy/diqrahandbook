# Legal & Compliance Control

Ensuring that Diqra operates within the legal frameworks of all guest nations.

## 1. Contract Approval Protocol
No contract exceeding $100k may be executed without a "Legal Clear" status from the external counsel. All contracts must include:
*   Standard Liability Limitation (capped at 50% of fee).
*   Intellectual Property Retention clauses.

## 2. Government Tender Participation
Tender participation requires a separate "Strategic Review" to ensure the bond requirements do not compromise company liquidity.

## 3. Compliance Monitoring Checklist
*   Monthly VAT/GST reconciliation.
*   Annual Anti-Bribery & Corruption (ABC) training for all directors.
*   Environmental impact clearances for all industrial projects.

## 4. Documentation & Audit Trail
All project-related emails and financial documents must be archived on the "Project Vault" for a minimum of 7 years.
