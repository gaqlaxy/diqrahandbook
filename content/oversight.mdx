# Project Oversight Protocol

Architecture and infrastructure projects carry significant liability. This protocol defines the oversight required to mitigate risks.

## 1. Project Acceptance Criteria
Before signing any new contract, the project must meet 3 of 5 criteria:
1.  **Strategic Alignment**: Within core competencies (Residential, Commercial, Infra).
2.  **Profitability**: Projected net margin > 15%.
3.  **Creditworthiness**: Client has no history of legal disputes or payment defaults.
4.  **Resource Availability**: Internal team has the capacity (no > 80% utilization).
5.  **Brand Impact**: High visibility or landmark status.

## 2. Risk Categorization Matrix
| Level | Impact | Approval Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P1** | Global/Public Scale | Full Board |
| **P2** | Complex Commercial | Functional Director |
| **P3** | Standard Residential | Project Lead |

## 3. High-Risk Project Approval Rules
Any project involving offshore tunneling, toxic waste remediation, or government debt financing is automatically **P1** and requires a third-party risk audit.

## 4. Escalation Hierarchy
*   **Operational Issue**: Handled by Senior Architect.
*   **Budget Overrun (>5%)**: Escalate to Finance Director.
*   **Safety Incident / Legal Notice**: Immediate escalation to Managing Director.

## 5. Dispute Handling Protocol
All contracts must include a Mandatory Arbitration clause. Legal representation must be notified within 24 hours of any "Notice of Dispute".
