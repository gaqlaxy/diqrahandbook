# Digital & Cybersecurity Control

Infrastructure architecture is high-value intellectual property. Digital security is as critical as physical site safety.

## 1. BIM Data Integrity
Building Information Modeling (BIM) files represent a digital twin of critical infrastructure.
*   **Access Control**: MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication) required for all cloud-hosted model repositories.
*   **Version Lock**: No production model can be modified without a "Commit Log" identifying the requester and reason.

## 2. Client Confidentiality
*   **Secret Projects**: Use code-names for high-security infrastructure projects.
*   **Clean Desk Policy**: No physical drawings of sensitive sites (e.g., Banks, Power Plants) may be left unsecured overnight.

## 3. Cyber-Incident Response
*   In the event of a data breach or ransomware:
    *   **T+0**: Disconnect all internal servers from the public internet.
    *   **T+1h**: Notify the Board and IT Security Director.
    *   **T+4h**: Establish "Golden Image" restoration from physical backups.

## 4. Hardware Security
*   No external USB drives allowed on BIM workstations.
*   Encrypted laptops required for all Directors and Site Leads.
