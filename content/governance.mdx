# Corporate Governance Framework

This framework ensures that Diqra is managed with the highest level of professional ethics and legal compliance.

## 1. Board Structure
The Board of Directors consists of Executive and Non-Executive members. 
*   **Executive Directors**: Responsible for day-to-day operations and strategic execution.
*   **Non-Executive Directors**: Provide independent oversight and specialized industry expertise.

## 2. Director Roles & Responsibilities
*   **Managing Director**: Final decision-maker on capital allocation and international expansion.
*   **Technical Director**: Approval authority for engineering standards and project delivery protocols.
*   **Financial Director**: Oversight of audit trails, tax compliance, and cash flow stability.

## 3. Voting Protocols
*   **Simple Majority**: Required for operational decisions (e.g., vendor swaps).
*   **Supermajority (75%)**: Required for asset liquidation, major debt acquisition, or brand rebranding.

## 4. Delegation of Authority (DoA) Matrix
The DoA defines who can sign what. 
*   **Expenditure < $50k**: Department Head approval.
*   **Expenditure $50k – $500k**: Functional Director approval.
*   **Expenditure > $500k**: Board approval required.

## 5. Conflict of Interest Policy
All directors must disclose potential conflicts annually. Any director with a personal interest in a vendor or client must recuse themselves from related voting sessions.
