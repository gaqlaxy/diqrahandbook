# Brand & Reputation Control

Diqra’s reputation is a multi-million-dollar asset. It must be protected via strict communication controls.

## 1. Media Handling Policy
Directors are the only authorized spokespeople for the firm. Any media inquiry must be logged and vetted by the Brand Director before a response is issued.

## 2. Public Statement Protocol
All press releases or public project reveals must be reviewed for:
*   Confidentiality (Client NDA check).
*   Political Neutrality.
*   Scientific Accuracy.

## 3. Social Media Governance
Directors' personal social media accounts must carry a disclaimer that views are personal. No project-in-progress photos may be posted without client approval.

## 4. Client Dispute Escalation
Client complaints reaching the "Senior Management" level must be resolved within 48 hours to prevent online reputational damage.
