# Sustainability & ESG Governance

Diqra is committed to carbon neutrality and ethical infrastructure delivery.

## 1. Environmental Benchmarks
*   **Design Phase**: Minimum LEED Gold or BREEAM Excellent equivalent for all projects > $10M.
*   **Operational Phase**: Mandatory Embodied Carbon calculation during the design development phase.

## 2. Social Impact Monitoring
Every infrastructure project must involve a "Community Liaison" phase to ensure local stakeholders are consulted. Directors must review the Social Impact Assessment (SIA) before initial site mobilization.

## 3. Governance on Ethical Materials
*   **No Conflict Materials**: Supply chain audit required for all heavy rare-earth metals and structural steel.
*   **Fair Labor**: Zero-tolerance for forced labor in the subcontracting chain. Periodic on-site audits are mandatory for all Tier-1 vendors.

## 4. Carbon Offsetting & R&D
Diqra allocates 2% of annual net profit to "Green Tech R&D", focusing on low-carbon concrete and solar-integrated facades.
