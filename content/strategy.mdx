# Strategic Review Framework

The Board conducts a deep-dive review every 90 days to ensure performance aligns with the Executive Charter.

## 1. Quarterly Board Review Checklist
*   Review P&L versus budget.
*   Audit DoA compliance (Were any signatures missed?).
*   Assess Project Risk Matrix (Are any P1 projects currently delayed?).
*   Review Director Performance & Participation.

## 2. KPI Dashboard Structure
Directors monitor 4 core indexes:
1.  **Liquidity Ratio**: Current assets / Current liabilities. (Healthy: > 1.5).
2.  **Utilization Rate**: Billable hours / Total hours. (Healthy: 75% - 85%).
3.  **Client NPS**: Net Promoter Score from finalized infrastructure projects.
4.  **Compliance Score**: External audit findings.

## 3. Performance Review Model
Directors are reviewed based on:
*   Strategic Contribution.
*   Risk Mitigation.
*   Talent Development.

## 4. Expansion Approval Model
Entering a new geographical market or infrastructure vertical requires:
*   Competitor Analysis Report.
*   Tax & Legal Nexus Study.
*   Local Partnership Validation.
*   Board approval by Supermajority (75%).
