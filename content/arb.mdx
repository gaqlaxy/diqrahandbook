# Architecture Review Board (ARB)

The ARB is the supreme technical authority within Diqra, ensuring that every project aligns with our "Integrated Excellence" standards.

## 1. Composition of the ARB
*   **Chair**: Technical Director or Chief Architect.
*   **Members**: Functional Heads of Structural Engineering, Sustainability, and Digital Delivery.
*   **Peer Reviewers**: Rotating Senior Lead from an un-related project.

## 2. Review Gateways
Every project must pass through 3 mandatory ARB "Gates":
1.  **Gate 1 (Concept Validation)**: Review of strategic design intent and site feasibility.
2.  **Gate 2 (Technical Resolution)**: Detail engineering review and material specification audit.
3.  **Gate 3 (Delivery Readiness)**: Final tender package audit and risk mitigation sign-off.

## 3. Waiver Protocols
Deviating from Diqra Standards requires a formal "Architecture Waiver" signed by the Technical Director. Waivers are only granted for unique site constraints or innovative technological trials.

## 4. Design Ethics & Aesthetics
The ARB reserves the right to veto designs that:
*   Compromise the Diqra "Visual Signature".
*   Utilize non-sustainable or unverified high-risk materials.
*   Fail to meet the 50-year lifecycle durability requirement.
