# Crisis Management Playbook

Rapid response protocols for non-standard, high-impact events.

## 1. Site Accident Handling
In the event of a major site accident:
*   **T+0**: Stop all work; call emergency services.
*   **T+1h**: Notify Technical Director & Legal counsel.
*   **T+4h**: Draft internal memo; secure site for investigation.

## 2. Financial Emergency
*   **Protocol**: If cash reserves drop < 3 months overhead, all non-essential capital expenditure is frozen immediately.
*   **Action**: Board meets within 24 hours to review credit lines.

## 3. Legal Notice Handling
*   All subpoena or "Stop Work Order" notices must be scanned and sent to the Board group within 60 minutes of receipt.

## 4. PR Crisis
*   Silent Mode: No director or employee may issue a statement until the PR Crisis team has vetted a "Holding Statement".
